HARDWARE DIAGNOSTIC SUITE

Interactive Troubleshooter

Select the symptom you are experiencing for an instant diagnostic check.

Select Issue Symptom:
Diagnosis: Audio Dropout or Latency Artifacts
  1. Check your Bluetooth Codec setting in the AeroSound companion app; if using 990kbps Sony LDAC in crowded RF environments, switch to "Best Effort (Adaptive)".
  2. Clear device pairing history by holding the Power and Volume (+) buttons simultaneously for 7 seconds until the LED pulses magenta.
  3. Verify that no other high-bandwidth USB 3.0 unshielded hubs are located within 30cm of your Bluetooth transmitter.
  4. Test the supplied 3.5mm silver-plated cable to isolate whether the issue is RF interference or the transducer diaphragm.
COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about AeroSound Series 3 Pro hardware and policies.

How do I pair AeroSound Series 3 with two devices at once (Multipoint)? ▼
Power on the headphones and hold the pairing button for 3 seconds to connect device #1. Once paired, hold the button again until the LED flashes blue to pair device #2. Both devices remain synchronously linked, automatically handing over priority when a phone call arrives.
Can I listen via USB-C or 3.5mm while the battery is depleted? ▼
Yes. The 3.5mm input features an analog passive bypass mode that operates with 0% battery power directly from any DAC or studio interface. When connected via USB-C to a PC or Mac, the internal 24-bit/96kHz DAC powers up while simultaneously charging.
What is covered under the 2-Year International Warranty? ▼
The standard warranty covers 100% of driver voice-coil defects, battery capacity degradation below 80%, ANC microphone calibration drift, and internal PCB failure. We provide doorstep courier pickup and replacement within 48 hours worldwide.
How do I enable 990kbps Hi-Res Sony LDAC streaming? ▼
On Android 12+, navigate to Settings → Connected Devices → AeroSound Series 3 → toggle on "LDAC High Resolution Audio". In Android Developer Options, ensure Bluetooth Audio LDAC Playback Quality is locked to "Optimized for Audio Quality (990kbps/909kbps)".
What is the 30-day trial and return policy? ▼
We offer an unconditional 30-day listening trial. If you are not completely stunned by the beryllium acoustic fidelity or ANC comfort, initiate a return from this support hub for a prepaid return label and 100% refund.
Are the ear cushions replaceable and user-swappable? ▼
Yes. AeroSound ear cushions feature our patented quad-point neodymium magnetic alignment mechanism. Simply grasp the lambskin ring and pull gently away from the CNC cup to detach. New cushion pairs snap into place in seconds.
